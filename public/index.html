Before your test

Prepare right, get accurate results.

A few small things make a big difference to your report. When in doubt, ask us. We'll tell you exactly what to do for your test.

Fasting tests

Sugar, lipid & full-body

  • No food for 8–12 hours before the sample
  • Plain water is allowed, no tea, coffee or juice
  • Avoid alcohol and heavy, oily food the night before
Thyroid

T3, T4, TSH

  • Morning sample is preferred
  • If you take a thyroid tablet, ask your doctor whether to take it after the sample
  • Mention any biotin or multivitamin supplements
Urine

Routine & culture

  • First morning urine is best
  • Collect mid-stream in the sterile container we give
  • Wash hands; don't touch the inside of the lid
Everyone

On the day

  • Wear a loose or half-sleeve top
  • Keep your prescription and any old reports handy
  • Don't stop any medicine without your doctor's advice
